Privacy Policy — Momenty

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Momenty is a local-first mood journaling app. Privacy is fundamental to how it is built.

Summary

Momenty does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

What we store, and where

All of your data — moods, notes, photos, activity and person labels, your display name, and settings — is stored locally on your device. Momenty has no servers, no account system, and no login. We cannot see your data.

Photos

When you attach a photo to a day, Momenty asks permission to access your photo library. The photo you choose is copied into the app's local storage on your device. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Sharing

When you share a Wrapped card, the image is generated on your device and handed to your system share sheet. You decide whether and where to share it. Momenty does not send it anywhere on its own.

Analytics and tracking

Momenty contains no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising, and uses no third-party trackers.

Third parties

We do not share your data with third parties, because your data never leaves your device.

Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes all of your data. (This also means uninstalling the app without a backup permanently removes it.)

Children

Momenty is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
